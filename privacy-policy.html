Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026

AI Trainer ("the app") is built and operated by Kelvin Lee. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address and name when you sign up.
  • Training data: your goals, equipment, experience level, workout logs, and set records. This is the core data the app needs to generate and adapt your programme.
  • Device token: your Expo push notification token, used only to send training-related push notifications (weight updates, personal bests).

How we use it

  • To generate and personalise your training programme using AI (Anthropic Claude API).
  • To send push notifications about your training — weight adjustments and personal bests.
  • To show you your progress over time.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than operating the app.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland). Privacy policy.
  • Anthropic Claude API — AI programme generation. Your profile and workout data is sent to Claude to generate responses. Privacy policy.
  • Expo Push Notifications — push delivery. Privacy policy.

Data retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data by emailing kelvin.g.lee@gmail.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at kelvin.g.lee@gmail.com for any data requests.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We'll update this policy if our practices change. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
